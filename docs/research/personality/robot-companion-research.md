# Persistent character for BB8: embodied research and implementation review

Research cutoff: **24 September 2026**. Repository observations are snapshots read on that date, not promises about later releases. Scope: autonomous character that changes through experience, physical companion implementations, and whether robot foundation models solve the missing problem. No packages were installed, no hardware was contacted, and no BB8 or Genesis Studio source was changed.

**Recommendation:** retain BB8's verified navigation controller and build a small, persistent motivation-and-preference learner above it. Borrow expressive coordination from companion projects and task-memory mechanisms from current robotics research. A larger VLA is not the first missing component. The reviewed evidence supports adaptive preferences, state-dependent behavior and remembered consequences; it does not establish a downloadable model that develops a durable companion personality by itself.

## 1. Evidence closest to the actual objective

**Adaptive object preferences on Mini — September 2025.** Maroto-Gómez et al. update preferences through a Rescorla–Wagner prediction-error rule. Reward depends on internal need, object experience and repetition; expression depends on preference. The robot has simulated feeding, hydration and entertainment variables, RFID identification, speech, eye displays and five motion degrees of freedom. In the within-subject comparison, adaptive preferences plus emotion improved perceived animacy, intelligence and agency, but not likeability or sociability. The experiment standardized initial preferences and reset them between participants, whereas proposed long-term operation preserves them. Randomizing needs every 30 seconds accelerates the study; it is not a model of actual battery needs. This is a direct precedent for BB8's learned object/place preferences, without evidence of months-long personality development. Read: publisher sections 3.3–5 and limitations. The archive records 2 September 2025. [Paper](https://www.mdpi.com/2079-9292/14/18/3711), [institutional record](https://e-archivo.uc3m.es/entities/publication/abbbcbee-0f05-4fbb-a74f-5b592d73095f).

**HARMONY — accepted 2 August 2026.** Read the author manuscript's methods and evaluation, rather than relying on ResearchGate's January label. Tabular Q-learning adjusts speech, gesture and communication parameters, combining simulated-user pretraining with online adaptation. Reward uses estimated valence and detected emotion; simulated users encode designer-assumed preferences. On physical ARI, 46 participants received adaptive or random behavior. Sessions including warm-up/breaks lasted approximately 40 minutes. Adaptive behavior produced more conversation turns (27.95 versus 20.28), but neither self-reported affect nor perceived social intelligence improved significantly. The authors explicitly distinguish adaptive expressive cues from stable psychological personality. This strengthens the case for learning expression parameters, not for autonomous motives or longitudinal identity. BB8 would need its own motor vocabulary and more reliable outcome feedback than an emotion proxy. The accepted manuscript is CC BY 4.0; no complete author code release was verified. [Author manuscript, DOI 10.1109/LRA.2026.3726339](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/413453023_HARMONY_Multimodal_Spectrum-Based_Personality_Adaptation_for_Human-Robot_Interaction).

**Homeostatic RL — 2024–2025.** Yoshida, Sprekeler and Gutkin's July 2025 synthesis defines reward through reduction in deviation from internal setpoints. This makes an outcome's value depend on current need: the same resource can become attractive or uninteresting without replacing the policy. I read the mathematical drive/reward treatment and behavior discussion, rather than treating the article as a new robot benchmark. It supports a principled objective for energy, temperature and other operational variables; it does not prove that choosing synthetic drives produces feelings. [Full paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.04998). The related September 2024 Neural Networks work has [author code](https://github.com/ugo-nama-kun/homeostatic_agents_pfrl) using PFRL and older MuJoCo environment packages. That is a research reference, not a ready BB8 dependency; I did not verify a reusable license in the inspected repository. [2024 publication](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0893608024003034).

**Prosocial motivation needs an objective — NeurIPS workshop, 2024.** Yoshida and Man train recurrent PPO agents in food-sharing, one-dimensional and two-dimensional environments, comparing no empathy, observation of a partner's needs, coupled needs, and both. Across the reported 20-run conditions, merely observing a partner's state did not produce the tested helping behavior; coupling the partner's state into the homeostatic objective did. Read: methods, all three experiments and architecture appendix. For BB8, recognizing a person or labeling their emotion will not automatically teach consideration. User feedback and interaction boundaries must affect learned action values. The evidence is simulated and uses privileged partner-state information; it is not evidence that camera emotion recognition supplies reliable human needs. [Full paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.12103v1), [workshop version](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=efXcvMnqEl).

**Robot Character Generation — 2025, weaker than its headline.** Tang et al. combine Big Five prompts, appraisal and episodic-to-semantic reflection. Methods describe one author role-playing four scenarios and three personality settings, plus memory/emotion ablations. The limitations explicitly say experiments were simulated, physical deployment and a broader user study remain future work, and actions come from a defined action space. I read sections III–V and VII. This is useful memory-organization work, but neither policy learning nor strong physical-companion validation. BB8 should not present a personality prompt and generated autobiographical summary as learned character. [Full paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.15518v1).

**RoboBunting — accepted 24 March, online 10 April 2026.** Its 14-person study varies leader/follower role, stiffness, frequency and delay on a one-degree-of-freedom haptic device; social impressions change with physical dynamics. This suggests expression can arise from continuous interaction rather than only playback clips. However, only the publisher abstract and study summary were retrievable in this review; full methods were not verified. It is a secondary design lead, not a selected learning algorithm. BB8 lacks the paper's contact-force embodiment, so importing its impedance behavior would be unjustified. [Publisher record](https://doi.org/10.1145/3807949).

The useful separation is **learned motive → feasible skill → expressive execution**. A gesture library remains acceptable as a motor vocabulary if experience determines whether, when and how it is used. It becomes inadequate when its trigger table is the entire character. The same principle applies to a learned gait: replacing a clip with a neural policy does not itself create preferences or persistent identity.

Firsthand deployment reports add practical constraints, without establishing failure rates. In [Reachy issue #1402](https://github.com/pollen-robotics/reachy_mini/issues/1402), opened **8 September 2026**, a Wireless owner reports battery-operation servo torque loss while the daemon still appears healthy, including a ten-minute measurement. This is an unverified owner report, not a confirmed defect affecting every unit. It motivates separating commanded motion, measured motion and hardware health. In [wire-pod issue #531](https://github.com/kercre123/wire-pod/issues/531), opened **15 September 2026**, an Apple-Silicon user reports a Vector 2.0 Bluetooth handshake-version mismatch preventing pairing and proposes a handler fix. This illustrates integration/version fragility, not learning performance. Both issue bodies were read; neither proposed patch was executed or independently reproduced.

## 3. Foundation models execute tasks; personality remains a separate question

**π0.5 — April 2025.** Its hierarchy predicts subtasks and motor actions using heterogeneous robot/web supervision. The unseen-home experiment uses three homes, bedroom/kitchen tasks and ten trials per task/environment; the metric includes task progress, so it should not be silently translated into complete success. Read: architecture/co-training, evaluation and ablations. This is meaningful task decomposition and generalization, not an internally originated persistent purpose. BB8 has neither the mobile manipulator's arms nor its proprioceptive/action representation. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.16054v1).

**π0.6 and π*0.6/RECAP — November 2025.** The [17 November model card](https://website.pi-asset.com/pi06star/PI06_model_card.pdf) describes Gemma3-4B plus an approximately 860M action expert; its 63-ms example uses an H100, not a Mac. RECAP combines demonstrations, autonomous experience and interventions, trains value estimates, then conditions policy learning on advantage. Read: policy extraction, reward/value training and physical experiments. Iterative studies use bimanual manipulators on laundry, espresso and boxes; they measure throughput and human-annotated task success. The paper reports substantial improvements, but the task objectives remain supplied. Thus “learns from experience” is real policy improvement, not autonomous character formation. [RECAP paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.14759v1).

The audited [official openpi catalog](https://github.com/Physical-Intelligence/openpi) exposes π0, FAST and π0.5; I did not find a released π0.6/RECAP package there. It specifies NVIDIA GPUs and Ubuntu 22.04, with other operating systems unsupported. Code is Apache-2.0, and bundled [Gemma terms](https://github.com/Physical-Intelligence/openpi/blob/main/LICENSE_GEMMA.txt) also matter. A third-party repository named “pi0.6” is not evidence of an official checkpoint or equivalent training result.

**MEM — 4 March 2026.** This later π0.6-family work combines compact recent video with semantic text memory updated during task execution. I read the encoder, memory construction and physical evaluation, including hidden-object retrieval, mug sequencing, recipe setup and adaptation after failed grasps. It supports remembering failures and completed subtasks over up to fifteen-minute tasks. It does not establish cross-day identity learning: that is a different retention, consolidation and evaluation problem. Borrow its distinction between dense recent evidence and compact durable event summaries, while keeping numeric navigation belief separate from text memory. [Full paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.03596v1).

**SmolVLA — 2 June 2025.** The main paper model is 450M parameters, with a roughly 100M flow-matching action expert. Read: architecture, pretraining and experimental protocol. Evaluations use LIBERO/Meta-World and SO-100/SO-101 manipulation; physical tasks use demonstrations and graded subtask scores. The model is therefore the most plausible small VLA baseline among this shortlist if BB8 later needs demonstration-learned actions, not a pretrained BB8 policy or motivation engine. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.01844v1), [official implementation guide](https://huggingface.co/docs/lerobot/smolvla), [Apache-2.0 model card](https://huggingface.co/lerobot/smolvla_base).

**OpenVLA — June 2024.** Read: architecture, multi-robot evaluation and adaptation experiments. Its 7B policy predicts tokenized actions from visual/language input; evaluation concerns manipulation and transfer. Current maintainers recommend the newer OFT fine-tuning recipe. The [repository](https://github.com/openvla/openvla) is MIT, but weights inherit Llama-2 community terms. Its documented LoRA configuration uses NVIDIA hardware, with about 27GB minimum memory after batch adjustments; that is not a verified Apple Metal requirement. [Original paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.09246v1). For this project, the model size and embodiment adaptation burden have no demonstrated advantage over a small high-level preference learner.

**GR00T — N1 paper March 2025; N1.7 available by this review.** Read: original data strategy, post-training, physical experiments and limitations. The original full-data GR-1 tabletop result averages 76.8%, versus 46.4% for its diffusion-policy baseline; it is not a general autonomy score. [N1 paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.14734v1). Current [official code](https://github.com/NVIDIA/Isaac-GR00T) supports N1.7 and embodiment-specific data/action configurations. Its [release page](https://github.com/NVIDIA/Isaac-GR00T/releases) labels the 18 April N1.7 tag Early Access, whereas current main calls it GA: pin the exact code and checkpoint rather than mixing maturity claims. Code is Apache-2.0; the [model card](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/GR00T-N1.7-3B) specifies NVIDIA Open Model License. Neither humanoid end-effector actions nor its current inference stack transfer directly to BB8.

## 4. Selection for BB8 and the 48GB M5 Pro

My proposed first implementation is a persistent, small learner over BB8's existing verified skills, with four explicit layers:

1. **Operational state:** measured or honestly simulated energy, elapsed activity, localization validity and interaction availability. Hard constraints stay outside learned preferences.
2. **Memory:** timestamped observations, actions and outcomes; learned place/object familiarity and preference estimates with confidence. Store evidence separately from an optional language summary.
3. **Motivation and choice:** predict the outcome of feasible goals; learn their values from actual consequences and explicit user feedback. Slowly changing exploration/social-proximity preferences can produce individuality without hand-authoring every response.
4. **Expression:** modulate verified movement timing and any supported expressive channels according to state and intention. The controller continues to reject unsafe or unlocalized motion, regardless of character preference.

This is an architectural inference from the evidence, not an existing evaluated BB8 result. A small contextual value learner or recurrent high-level policy can test the crucial hypothesis without making a foundation model responsible for every wheel command. The supplied Mac's memory capacity is not proof of CUDA-stack compatibility or control latency. Benchmark any later SmolVLA candidate locally before choosing it; defer openpi/GR00T deployment until there is an embodiment dataset and a demonstrated compute benefit.

Evaluate actual persistence: reboot and return tomorrow; reverse an object's outcomes; move familiar objects; present unseen places; withhold feedback; compare learned values against a frozen/randomized baseline. Record adaptation speed, retention, inappropriate repetition, safety stops and user preference. An ablation with identical expression but learning disabled distinguishes acquired behavior from theatrical presentation. An ablation retaining memory while freezing updates distinguishes recall from learning. These tests address the user's objective much more directly than a successful dance, conversational demo or manipulation benchmark.

## Reading and uncertainty record

Full method/evaluation text was read for HARMONY, Tang, empathic coupling, SmolVLA, OpenVLA, π0.5, RECAP, MEM and GR00T N1; the 2025 homeostasis item is a synthesis, not a new benchmark. Mini's publisher-indexed method, study, results and limitation sections were read, but a downloadable training package was not verified. RoboBunting remains abstract-level evidence because full-text retrieval failed. The 2024 Neural Networks publisher was inaccessible directly; its author repository and 2025 synthesis were used only for the limited statements above. Repository READMEs, license files and the two cited issue bodies were inspected. No training, latency, hardware reliability or longitudinal companion claims were independently reproduced. Only material dated on or before the stated cutoff was admitted; floating repository branches must be pinned again before implementation.
