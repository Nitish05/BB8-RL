# Intrinsic motivation and persistent BB8 behavior: research assessment

Researched 24 September 2026. This review concerns BB8's existing simulated, camera-localized navigation stack: frozen SAC waypoint control, an estimated room map, and one to three fixed cameras. There is no implemented high-level personality agent. No models were installed or benchmarked. Hardware discussion concerns the stated Apple M5 Pro with 48 GB; it does not imply robot deployment or measured local performance.

## Recommendation and evidential standard

Implement a **persistent, experience-updated goal selector above the existing navigation controller**. Combine a small set of explicit internal needs, learned estimates of goal outcomes, and competence/learning-progress estimates. Preserve autobiographical event records and slowly changing preferences across sessions. Treat an LLM, if added, as a constrained proposal/explanation component. Do not start by retraining pixel-to-motor Dreamer or by labeling a prompt-generated mood as learned personality.

Operationally, a personality claim needs stable, repeatable differences in *choices*, with controlled evidence that accumulated experience changes those differences. A self-selected goal is weaker: an agent can choose its next objective while its goal vocabulary, intrinsic objective, and preferences remain entirely designer-defined. None of the methods below establishes spontaneous human-like needs or enduring individuality in BB8's setting.

## What the primary literature actually demonstrates

**DreamerV3 — Nature, 2 April 2025; initial preprint 2023.** I read the learning algorithm, Minecraft observations/rewards, benchmark and ablation sections. A recurrent world model learns dynamics, rewards and continuation; actor/critic training uses imagined trajectories, while deployed actions come from the actor without online search. The Minecraft achievement is substantial, but its methods specify twelve rewarded milestones, inventory inputs, health rewards and abstract crafting actions. Thus “no human curriculum” does not mean self-invented purpose. This is a future learner for predicting consequences of BB8's selected activities, not a ready personality module. [Paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08744-2)

**Director — NeurIPS 2022; preprint 8 June 2022.** I read manager/worker objectives, §3 experiments and §5 limitations. A manager chooses quantized latent goals; a worker receives a goal-reaching reward. Both train in imagination. It solves sparse-reward egocentric ant mazes, where flat exploration can fail. The manager nevertheless optimizes supplied task and exploration rewards; it invents *subgoals*, not terminal values. Fixed goal durations and representation-wide goals are acknowledged limitations. Reported training used a V100, under 24 hours per run, about 20% more computation than DreamerV2. Its hierarchy is relevant; replacing BB8's proven worker is unnecessary. [Paper](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2022/file/a766f56d2da42cae20b5652970ec04ef-Paper-Conference.pdf)

**Plan2Explore — ICML 2020.** I read §3 intrinsic objective/information-gain derivation, §4 setup and task-adaptation results. Five learned predictors disagree about next image embeddings; an exploration policy maximizes expected disagreement inside a world model. Experiments use image-only Control Suite tasks and three seeds. The reward-free exploration phase builds reusable knowledge, but downstream evaluation supplies task rewards. Disagreement approximates reducible uncertainty; it is not a calibrated risk bound. For BB8, borrow the distinction between learnable novelty and random visual noise. A blinking screen or camera failure must not become an attractive “interesting place.” [Paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v119/sekar20a/sekar20a.pdf)

**LEXA — NeurIPS 2021; preprint 18 October 2021.** I read explorer/achiever construction, temporal-distance reward, four-domain experiments and ablations. The explorer discovers surprising states; the achiever practices reaching them. Temporal distance helps avoid matching easy robot pixels while ignoring harder object changes. Tests use forty goal tasks and include sequential manipulation, but deployment goals are user-specified images. This provides real autonomous *practice-goal discovery*, not evidence of persistent preferences. BB8 can adopt an event/goal archive and learned reachability without learning a new pixel-space locomotion policy. [Paper](https://papers.nips.cc/paper/2021/file/cc4af25fa9d2d5c953496579b75f6f6c-Paper.pdf)

**MAGELLAN — ICML 2025; preprint 11 February, inspected revision 17 June 2025.** I read §3–4, goal-generation appendix, architecture ablations and compute budget. A goal-conditioned competence predictor estimates present and past success, allowing learning-progress sampling to generalize across related goals. In Little-Zoo, it handles an evolving goal space containing many impossible combinations. However, the environment is deterministic, fully observable, textual and episodic; its instruction/object grammar and success conditions are provided. This is compelling curriculum evidence, not open-ended robot identity. Replication is substantial: the appendix reports two H100 80 GB GPUs and 80 GPU-hours for a 500,000-episode seed using Flan-T5 250M and adapters. Borrow the small competence estimator and progress-driven selection, not this whole training system. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.07709v3)

**METRA — ICLR 2024; initial preprint 13 October 2023.** I read §4 objective/limitations and §5 experiments. Skills maximize displacement in a compact representation constrained by temporal adjacency, producing diverse locomotion and manipulation behaviors from pixels. This improves the repertoire available for later task selection. The skill prior, latent dimension and diversity objective are specified, and a repertoire alone does not decide what BB8 values. Its acknowledged issues with asymmetric dynamics matter when returning from an occluded region is harder than entering it. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.08887)

**Contrastive Successor Features, “Can a MISL Fly?” — ICLR 2025.** I read §5 algorithm, §6 comparisons, limitations and Appendix C.6. CSF replaces METRA's constrained representation training with a contrastive objective and learns vector-valued successor features. It performs roughly comparably on six continuous-control settings and provides a simpler future skill-learning baseline. Crucially, the authors report that both CSF and METRA lacked stable results on a five-room MiniHack task, hypothesizing difficulties from discrete actions, layout randomness and partial observability. This directly cautions against assuming skill discovery solves camera-occluded navigation. [Paper](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/4f0396df41b7ddcd10cafe7c16e0db85-Paper-Conference.pdf)

**Homeostatic RL — Keramati and Gutkin, eLife 2014.** I read the drive/reward equations, discounting argument, anticipatory responding and limitations. A physiological deficit function (D(h)) generates reward (D(h_t)-D(h_{t+1})); learned predictions can then produce anticipatory regulation instead of fixed reflexes. Setpoints, internal variables and their dynamics are assumed. The authors explicitly leave social and novelty rewards without a corresponding regulatory system unresolved. This supports making BB8's simulated energy or stimulation dynamics explicit; it does not scientifically justify calling an arbitrary “loneliness meter” a discovered biological need. [Paper](https://elifesciences.org/articles/04811)

**Unexpected Capability of Homeostasis for Open-ended Learning — ICDL 2025.** The full proceedings PDF/OSF download was inaccessible during this pass; I inspected the authors' project methods and executable environment reward code instead. Images plus health/food/drink/energy inputs drive a ResNet/RNN policy trained with asynchronous PPO. The author page reports five billion Crafter steps and behaviors including foraging, shelter building and escape. The environment code confirms weighted squared setpoint deviations, drive-reduction reward, and disabled achievement bonuses in homeostatic mode. This is stronger evidence of unlisted strategies emerging from specified needs, but still a designed ecology, not learned needs. [Author methods](https://sites.google.com/view/movingsloth/projects/unexpected-capability-of-homeostasis-for-open-ended-learning), [reward implementation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ugo-nama-kun/homeostatic_crafter/main/homeostatic_crafter/env.py), [proceedings DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICDL63968.2025.11204447)

The related 2025 paper **Homeostasis Driven Learning and Development in an Autonomous Robot**, John Lones, is a relevant lead, but I could not access primary methods or official executable code. Its reported Unitree Go2 claims of play/object permanence/self-soothing therefore do not support a recommendation here. The 2024 *Emergence of integrated behaviors through direct optimization for homeostasis* full text was also inaccessible; I have not treated its abstract as an inspected experiment. [Lones DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICDL63968.2025.11204417), [2024 DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neunet.2024.106379)

**Active inference / pymdp — 2022 paper, actively revised software through 2026.** I read the worked generative-model example and policy/learning functions. Expected free energy includes preference utility and information gain about hidden states or model parameters. The supplied `C` preference vector defines desirable outcomes; active inference does not remove reward/preference design. It is particularly useful as an interpretable high-level baseline for deciding between gathering information, revisiting a resource and resting. A compact discrete model can sit over navigation skills; uncertain camera poses must stay uncertain rather than becoming categorical facts. [Methods tutorial](https://arxiv.org/html/2201.03904)

**AXIOM — preprint 30 May 2025.** I read object-mixture architecture, planning, Gameworld evaluation and limitations. Online Bayesian structure growth and reduction support data-efficient control within 10,000 interactions. But the ten games deliberately use simple colored sprites, and the paper explicitly identifies engineered object/dynamics priors as a limitation. It is an interesting structured world-model alternative, not demonstrated RGB-room personality or a replacement for BB8's calibrated perception. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.24784v1)

**Social/autotelic evidence.** Nisioti et al., CoLLAs 2023, learn decentralized goal coordination and specialization in cooperative landmark navigation; I read the goal/reward formalization, coordination game and alignment ablations. Independent goal sampling fails where cooperation requires compatible goals. The goal space and goal rewards remain specified. Pitliya et al., August 2025, show active-inference cooperation in two 3×3-grid tasks; I read §2–4. Agent positions are perfectly observed, and preferred outcomes are given. Neither establishes lasting attachment to a real person. They support learning interaction contingencies once BB8 has reliable identity/interaction observations. [CoLLAs paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v232/nisioti23a/nisioti23a.pdf), [2025 methods](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.00401v1)

**Newest item checked: Belikov, 15 September 2026.** I read failure-mode discussion and §16 research agenda. This preprint examines intrinsic-objective local optima and proposes resource-constrained and hierarchical experiments. It is a perspective/proposed research programme, not evidence that a newer trained agent already solves this problem. Recency should not displace stronger experimental work. [Preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.17325v1)

Firsthand engineering reports qualify, rather than establish, performance: [DreamerV3 issue 152](https://github.com/danijar/dreamerv3/issues/152), opened 3 September 2024, describes a user's 200M custom-task RTX3090 run failing after about twelve hours despite apparently stable metrics. This is an anecdote, not proof that every 24 GB configuration fails. The pymdp maintainer's [issue 219](https://github.com/infer-actively/pymdp/issues/219), 19 August 2025, requests rollout equivalence tests and is now closed; current numerical/dependency fixes reinforce the need for version-pinned regression tests. Neither supports extrapolating throughput to the M5 Pro.

## A bounded BB8 experiment that would test the claim

The following is my proposed synthesis, not a result reported by a cited paper.

1. **Separate traits, needs and memories.** Traits are slowly changing priors over novelty, persistence and social opportunity. Needs are explicit evolving variables with documented dynamics. Memories store observed events, actions, prediction errors, completed goals and rejected goals. Store all three persistently with versions; restarting should not create a different individual. Label virtual battery/stimulation variables as simulated.
2. **Learn consequences over existing options.** Options initially mean navigate to a certified location, inspect, revisit, return to a designated rest/resource area, or wait. Learn distributions of duration, success, need change and information gained from actual observations. Update a small Bayesian/tabular or neural competence predictor. Generate candidate goals from remembered observations and reachable map regions, never simulator labels or imagined free space.
3. **Select goals from anticipated outcomes.** Compare predicted need reduction, competence progress, novelty and learned preference, minus time/effort and switching costs. Preserve separate logged terms. This is a configurable architecture, not an unearned claim that a weighted sum is the best theory of personality. Use a discrete active-inference selector as an ablation with the same observations/options.
4. **Preserve control authority.** Personality can request goals, not lower clearance, suppress Stop, redefine arrival or revive an expired pose. Lost localization cancels the request; recovery alone must not resume it. Persist rejected outcomes so the selector learns feasibility, while distinguishing perception failure from motor incompetence. Fixed cameras do not acquire new viewpoints simply because BB8 moves.
5. **Test causal individuality.** Compare random feasible goals, fixed needs rules, novelty-only, learning-progress-only, and the combined persistent learner. Run matched seeds with different experience histories, then identical probe situations. Swap or erase memory while holding traits fixed; swap traits while holding history fixed. Test satiation, resource relocation, impossible goals, noisy distractors, prolonged camera loss and process restart. Measure need stability, verified goal completion, repertoire growth, preference persistence, recovery and safety rejections—not expressive animations or an LLM's personality questionnaire.

A positive first result would be a reproducible change in self-selected activities caused by remembered outcomes, with intact navigation safeguards. Richer world models and learned motor skills should follow only if these inexpensive tests demonstrate a benefit that the current map-and-option representation cannot provide.
